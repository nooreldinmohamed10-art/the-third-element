What we treat

Focused care for the joints that carry you.

From post-injury recovery to return-to-sport programs, our sessions are built around four core areas.

Shoulder

Rotator cuff pain, impingement, post-surgical mobility and stiffness after immobilization.

Hip

Impingement, labral irritation, post-op rehab and hip-driven pain that shows up in the knee or back.

Spine

Low back and neck pain, disc-related symptoms, posture-driven strain and sciatica.

Sports injuries

Ligament and muscle injuries, return-to-play programming, and performance-focused strength work.
How we work

One path, three stages.

Every plan follows the same rhythm — no matter which joint brought you in.

01

Assess

A full movement and history review to find the actual source of pain, not just where it hurts.

02

Treat

Hands-on manual therapy paired with targeted exercise, adjusted session to session.

03

Restore

A return-to-function or return-to-sport plan, so progress holds after you leave the clinic.
